Frequently asked questions

Before choosing
a meal plan.

Key details to review before selecting a hostel dining option.

Is the hostel mess vegetarian?

LPU’s residence page lists vegetarian meals in university hostels. A non-vegetarian option is listed nearby through an independent vendor.

Is a mess plan compulsory?

LPU says students may choose a university mess plan or manage meals independently. Confirm the terms that apply to your residence booking.

Where can I find the daily menu?

LPU publishes a sample weekly menu. Check your hostel notice or student portal for the current daily selection.

Is the a la carte plan available in every hostel?

No. LPU notes that a la carte and some regional cuisines are available only at selected locations, subject to minimum student enrolment.
